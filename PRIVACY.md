# Privacy

Last changed November 15, 2026.

## What SAR Duty keeps

SAR Duty copies your team's data from D4H to run its tools for your team. Your team
decides what is in D4H.

Your team is responsible for its members' data. SAR Duty handles that data for your team
as a service provider.

SAR Duty also keeps the email or phone number you log in with. For each passkey you add,
it keeps the passkey's public key, its name, and when you added and last used it. Face ID
and fingerprints stay on your device. It keeps records of each login and each try, with
the email or number typed and the IP address, for 90 days.

SAR Duty keeps a certificate a member sends only until a team admin accepts or declines
it.

## Who sees it

Anyone with a member's ID card code can see the member's name, photo, and team on the
verify site. An app that scans the card gets the same answer, with the member's ID in D4H
or SAR Duty Records, which opens nothing without the team's own key.

On a team with member logins on, members see each other's names, photos, attendance,
groups, and qualifications. Phone numbers, addresses, emails, and emergency contacts stay
with team admins.

## Where it lives

SAR Duty does not sell data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. Its cookies keep you
logged in.

SAR Duty's server and database are in Canada. The other services it uses can be
anywhere, and get only what they need.

## Who runs SAR Duty

Gerry Shaw, a volunteer member of South Fraser Search and Rescue, runs SAR Duty. SAR Duty
is an independent project. South Fraser Search and Rescue and the other teams that use it
do not run, own, or endorse it.

SAR Duty site admins can see every team's data in SAR Duty. There are 2 today: Gerry Shaw
and Andrew Wallwork. They look at team data only to run, fix, support, secure, or improve
SAR Duty, or to look into a security problem, misuse, or failure. They share it only when
the law requires it. SAR Duty does not use team data for anything else.

## AI writing

A team admin can ask SAR Duty's AI writing to write a report. SAR Duty sends Anthropic's
Claude API, in the United States, the report's query, what the admin asked, and the
team's names for groups, qualifications, tags, and custom fields. It never sends members
or their records.

→ [How agents work with SAR Duty](https://sarduty.com/agents)

## Demos and organizations

Try a demo asks for no email. A name, if you give one, is kept on your demo until it is
deleted, 3 days after you first open it. Its made-up team's records are kept in SAR Duty
Records, in Canada, and deleted with it.

When a team belongs to an organization, its organization admins, such as an
association's staff, see each of its teams' totals, never members' names or contact
details.

## Your data

To see, fix, or remove your data, ask your team. Most of it comes from D4H, so the change
is made there. For anything else, email
[privacy@sarduty.com](mailto:privacy@sarduty.com).
